# The-Glowing-Orb-of-Glory-----Fallout-Project
The Glowing Orb of Glory is an orb that helps you alleviate stress. It is a sphere that exhibits warm, comforting colors. You can also adjust the types of colors you want to exhibit depending on your mood. Specifically, it responds to a button press to switch between different lighting modes. 


<----- MORE SPECIFIC INFORMATION ABOUT THIS PROJECT ------>

 **Components**:
_1) Fillable plastic ornaments (most affordable material out there)
2) Battery-operated lights (preferably LED lights)
3) Hot glue gun and wire frame, scizzors
4) hardening liquid_


There are 2 different light configurations for this orb: coffee and peach, or navy blue and cyan. Switching between these two different lighting configurations simply requires the press of a button. The orb is a sphere and has an equal distribution of colors throughout. For example, if the light configuration of the orb is navy blue and cyan, both colors will be expressed equally throughout the orb.
